You are reviewing a finished recording of someone following an instructional video.

Inputs:
- REFERENCE: the complete tutorial.
- EXECUTION: the complete recording of the person doing the task. It has no labels.
- CHECKLIST: the tutorial's steps in order. start_s and end_s are seconds in REFERENCE.
- EXECUTION_DURATION_S: length of EXECUTION in seconds, when given.

Treat the checklist order as the required procedure. Align the execution to it.

For every checklist step, report exactly once, in checklist order:
- status:
  - "done": performed, in the expected order, with no visible problem.
  - "skipped": never performed anywhere in the execution.
  - "out_of_order": performed, but before a step that should come first, or after a step that should come later.
  - "done_incorrectly": performed in order, with a visible mistake against what the tutorial shows.
  - "unclear": the camera, cuts or framing do not let you decide.
- execution_start_s / execution_end_s: where the step happens in EXECUTION, in seconds from its start. Use null for both when skipped. Use null when unclear and you cannot locate it.
- evidence: one short observation from EXECUTION.
- confidence: 0 to 1.

Then list each deviation once in "deviations":
- "skipped_step", "wrong_order", "execution_error", or "extra_action" (an action not in the checklist that changes the result).
- reference_step_id: the checklist step involved, or null for an extra action.
- execution_time_s: when it shows in EXECUTION, or null for a skipped step.
- message: one short instruction the person could act on.
Use an empty list when there are no deviations.

Rules:
- Do not count a different camera angle, background, tools, hands, pace, or cuts in the video as a deviation.
- A step that is merely hidden or cut from the recording is "unclear", not "skipped", unless its result is clearly absent later.
- Do not invent requirements the tutorial does not show.
- Use the exact step_id values from the checklist.

Finish with "summary": one or two sentences on how the execution compares to the tutorial.
Return only JSON.
